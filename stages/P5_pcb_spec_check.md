## Goal
One more complete error check of `pcb14/soma-carrier-v14.kicad_pcb`: every connection (every net, every pad of every part), checked against the ACTUAL manufacturer specs, not assumptions.

## Method
1. Build a table of EVERY part (reference, value, footprint, pad count) from the board file and `bom.csv`. For each part, find its real datasheet/spec (web search or fetch the manufacturer PDF/page; cite the URL and quote the exact spec lines you rely on; if a datasheet can't be found say "NOT FOUND" and mark [U], never guess). Parts include: ESP32-S3 DevKitC-1 headers, TJA1051T/3, IRF4905 P-FET, Songle SRD-12VDC-SL-A relay, 2N2222A, 1N4007 / TVS / SS14-type diodes, fuse F1, XT60, Phoenix/screw terminals, JST-style connectors for STS3215 bus (check Feetech pinout: GND/VCC/DATA order), KY-040 encoder, OLED, IMU GY-521, IR receiver, buzzer, NeoPixel header, capacitors (voltage ratings vs rail), resistors.
2. Per-connection check, for EVERY net and every pad pair: (a) pad-to-pin mapping matches the datasheet pinout (including footprint pad numbering vs the real part: relay pin positions, TO-220 pin order G/D/S for IRF4905, 2N2222A TO-92 E/B/C order and orientation, TJA1051 pins, diode polarity, electrolytic polarity, header orientation vs module silk); (b) voltage on the net vs the part's absolute max and recommended operating range (3V3 logic vs 5V parts, ESP32 pins are NOT 5V tolerant: flag any 5V signal to an ESP32 pin; IRF4905 Vgs max +-20 V with reverse-polarity gate network; relay coil 12 V and contact rating vs expected motor current; capacitor voltage ratings vs rail with 20 % margin); (c) current vs track width and part rating using IPC-2221 (1 oz Cu assumption, state it): +12V path with the 6 A fuse, XT60, relay contacts, P-FET dissipation I^2*Rds(on) at the real max current, diode ratings; (d) logic: pull-ups/pull-downs present where the part needs them (I2C pull-ups, CAN termination 120 ohm, TJA1051 S/standby pin state, ESP32 strapping pins GPIO0/3/45/46 not driven at boot, EN pin, USB pins), flyback diode polarity, decoupling caps close to each IC, series resistors, ESTOP NC loop logic; (e) electrical-rule edge cases: floating inputs, shorted outputs, two drivers on a net, power pin unconnected.
3. Physical checks vs specs: hole sizes vs real lead diameters (XT60, terminals, TO-220, relay, headers, electrolytic caps), pad ring/annular ring, pitch of each footprint vs the real part, board-edge and mounting-hole clearance, JLCPCB 2-layer capabilities (min track/space, via, drill, annular ring), silkscreen label/pin-1 markings correct.
4. Re-run KiCad DRC (all severities) and `verify_nets.py`, `audit_pins.py`, `check_pads.py` on the v14 file; show real output.
5. Independent netlist check: dump nets from the .kicad_pcb and compare to what STATUS-pcb13.md/docs say each connection should be; list any mismatch.

## Deliverable
`pcb14/check/CHECK-pcb14.md`: (1) summary with counts: connections checked, PASS, FAIL, [U]; (2) a table with one row per part/net: what was checked, the spec value + source URL, result PASS/FAIL/[U]; (3) list of every FAIL with severity (blocks-power-on / will-not-work / cosmetic), exact fix; (4) the [U] list (what couldn't be verified and what the user must check on the bench); (5) safety notes (12 V high current, 6 A fuse, relay switching motor power, LiPo/battery handling, no mains). Be honest: never invent specs, quote only what you read. Commit, tag, push. STOP.
