Give each part its own physical volume so it still gets its own material and its own result row. If fragmenting fails on the STEP/STL-derived solids, instead merge the printed parts of each rigid sub-assembly into one solid and glue sub-assemblies with `*EQUATION`/`*RIGID BODY` at the actuator output faces, and say so in the status.

Vendor parts (actuators, servos, racks, gear) are stiff blocks (E = 70 GPa) or rigid bodies with their real mass: they are not judged for stress. Use linear tets (C3D4) first; quadratic only if the first solve works and time is left.
