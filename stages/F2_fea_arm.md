## Goal
One stress analysis of the complete arm, not single parts. Show where the whole arm is stressed when it holds a payload at full reach, as a rotatable 3D model with a colour gradient like Fusion.

## Steps
1. Geometry: build the whole-arm assembly in its posed state from the sim model (`~/Vector-wt/sim/sim/` meshes + `STATUS-sim.md` joint positions) and the `cad/` generator/STEP files. Use the printed parts (links, flanges, spacers, cradles, base) as real solids, and the vendor parts (actuators, STS3215/3250 servos, bearings, racks) as simplified solid blocks or rigid bodies of the right size and mass. Copy inputs into `fea_arm/input/`.
2. Connections: parts are bolted together, so glue touching parts with tie/rigid connections at the joint interfaces (CalculiX `*TIE` or `*EQUATION`/rigid-body at bolt faces). Actuator output to link counts as one connected interface (no free rotation: the joints are held at the chosen pose). Say in the status exactly how each joint was modelled.
3. Materials [assumptions, flag as unverified in the status]: printed parts PETG-CF E = 4.0 GPa, nu = 0.35, working limit 25 MPa; vendor blocks steel/aluminium-like E = 70 GPa, nu = 0.33 (they are not judged for stress, only for stiffness and mass).
4. Pose and loads: fix the base plate bottom. Load case A: arm fully stretched horizontal (J2 90°, J3 0°, the longest reach pose; check against the joint ranges in `STATUS-sim.md`), gravity on the true part masses, plus 0.5 kg payload at the gripper tip. Load case B: the same at 2x. Load case C: the arm at the home pose of the demo, gravity + 0.5 kg payload.
5. Solve with CalculiX. Mesh: coarse tets, global size about 6 mm for the whole arm, refined to about 2–3 mm only on printed parts if time allows; run a coarse mesh first and report max stress for coarse vs refined. Report literal numbers: for each case the max von Mises stress overall, the max displacement at the gripper tip, and a table with, for every printed part, max von Mises and safety factor against 25 MPa. Also report the reaction torque at the base and compare the J2 and J3 reaction moments with the rated 3.5 and 2.25 N·m.
6. Viewer: `fea_arm/view_arm_stress.py` opens an interactive rotatable window (PyVista or FreeCAD GUI) of the whole arm with the von Mises colour map and displacement exaggerated ×20, with a case switch or one run command per case; put the exact commands in the status. Also render `fea_arm/arm_caseA.png`, `arm_caseB.png`, `arm_caseC.png` with a colour bar in MPa, front and side. Test that the viewer opens (launch, `screencapture`, kill).
7. List the top 5 highest-stress regions on the whole arm, each with a suggestion (fillet, thicker wall, stronger material, a stronger actuator). Do NOT modify the CAD.
