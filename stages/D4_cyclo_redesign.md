## Problem (from `verify/STATUS-ver.md`, branch `arm-ver`)
Hertz hand check says the printed PETG-CF cycloidal disc lobes FAIL at J2/J3 service torque (SF 0.14–0.33 vs 50 MPa along-layer), output-pin bearing at J2 marginal (SF 1.4–2.1). FEA never completed. Geometry: 40-lobe disc (4 mm thick, two discs per module), 41 ring pins Ø3 @ R23.5, 6 output pins Ø3 PCD 33, eccentricity 0.42 mm, clearance ~0.15 mm, housing 57 mm OD, 40:1. Code in `cad/act2/`, params in `cad/act2/` and `verify/code/hertz_cyc.py`. Slim is a hard user preference: do not enlarge the actuator OD; axial length growth must be justified and small.

## Tasks
1. **Proper cycloidal load-distribution model** (`cyclo4/load_model.py`): implement the standard analytic method (e.g. Blanche & Yang 1989 / Hidaka-style) with real disc profile from the CAD parameters, including the 0.15 mm clearance/profile modification and the rolling-pin radius: compute, for a given output torque, the number of pins in contact, each contact force, and peak contact force per disc as function of angle. Validate on a case with published numbers (cite). Then compute Hertz contact pressure (pin vs flank curvature from the real profile), sub-surface and root bending stress, and output-pin bearing stress at 3.5/2.25 N·m rated, ×2 dynamic, and the shock peaks (J2 9.19, J3 4.21 N·m). Also compute the actual maximum torque the motor can deliver at the gearbox output: 5 A [U] × Kt 0.0289 × 40 × 0.7 [U] ≈ 4.05 N·m, and state which loads can exceed that (back-driving by gravity/shock) and what protects against them.
2. **Options (evaluate each with the same model, keep OD ≤ 57 mm):** (a) thicker discs or 3-disc stack; (b) tighter clearance with a profile mod and a different tooth count that raises the number of contacts; (c) steel ring-pin rollers (needle/dowel rollers) sizes available in the UK; (d) disc material: PA-CF / PAHT-CF (A1 limits!), PETG-CF, nylon, acetal insert lobes, or a laser/water-jet/wire-cut STEEL or aluminium 3–4 mm disc from a UK cutting service (find at least 2 real UK services with a real price per part or a price-calculator reading, URL, date); (e) different ratio (e.g. 30:1 or 25:1 with the same motor) and what it does to torque, speed and the 0.5 kg payload target (use `upgrade1/gravity_up1.py`); (f) a bought planetary/strain-wave gearhead or a bought joint module as a fallback for J2/J3 only, with real UK price (e.g. a small harmonic/planetary gearbox that fits slim), only to report the cost difference.
