## What changes vs v1.1 (nothing else changes)
1. NEW IC **U2 = TJA1051T/3** CAN transceiver, footprint `Package_SO.pretty/SOIC-8_3.9x4.9mm_P1.27mm.kicad_mod`, placed on F.Cu. Pins: 1 TXD=`CAN_TX`, 2 GND, 3 VCC=`+5V_MCU`, 4 RXD=`CAN_RX`, 5 VIO=`+3V3`, 6 CANL=`CANL`, 7 CANH=`CANH`, 8 S=`GND` (always normal mode).
2. NEW `C5`, `C6` 100 nF (same footprint as existing C2), one from `+5V_MCU` to GND next to U2 pin 3, one from `+3V3` to GND next to U2 pin 5, each within 6 mm of the pin.
3. NEW `J17` CAN connector: same Molex KK-254 3-pin footprint as J4, pins 1=GND, 2=CANH, 3=CANL, at a board edge.
4. NEW `JP2` 2-pin 2.54 mm header (same footprint as JP1) in series with NEW `R9` 120 Ω (same footprint as R1): CANH → R9 → JP2 → CANL (a jumper that adds the bus termination).
5. NEW `J18` 12 V output for the motor controller boards: same Phoenix 2-pin terminal as J2, pins 1=`+12V`, 2=GND, on a board edge, fed from the +12V rail AFTER fuse F1, track width 2.5 mm.
6. Change `F1` value text from "6A" to "10A" (same footprint). Keep all `+12V*` tracks at 2.5 mm.
7. GPIO: rename net `SP1` → `CAN_TX` and `SP2` → `CAN_RX` on ESP32 socket positions `u1a_map[18]` (IO2) and `u1a_map[19]` (IO1). Remove SP1/SP2 from J15 pins 3 and 4 (leave those pads with no net). In `ROUTES` delete the two `SP1`/`SP2` lines and add routes `CAN_TX` U1A.18→U2.1 and `CAN_RX` U1A.19→U2.4 (0.25 mm). Add `CAN_TX`, `CAN_RX`, `CANH`, `CANL` to the net list (near line 39) and to the PWR groups as group 3.
8. Everything else (all other parts, nets, pin map, 135 × 115 mm outline) stays exactly as v1.1. The board may grow at most to 135 × 125 mm if J17/J18/U2 do not fit; if so say so in the status.

2. `cp pcb/gen_pcb.py pcb/fill_zones.py pcb/pour.py pcb/board_view.py pcb/check_pads.py pcb/audit_pins.py pcb/fab.sh pcb/README.md pcb12/`. 3. Make changes 1–8 only in `pcb12/gen_pcb.py` (copy of the generator). Use the same `place()` calls as the neighbouring parts. Placement rules: U2 within 40 mm of ESP32 socket pins U1A.18/19 and within 25 mm of J17; keep ≥ 1.5 mm pad-to-pad from other nets; put J17, J18 on the bottom or right edge with ≥ 3 mm to the edge. Use `board_view.py` to find free space BEFORE choosing coordinates. SMD pads on F.Cu: the router treats them like pads; if the A* cannot reach U2 pins, write the routes by hand in `ROUTES` waypoints (ROUTING_GUIDE 3h, 0/90° segments, 0.25 mm), CANH/CANL side by side 0.3 mm apart.

5. Pass criteria (print literal numbers in the status): 0 `shorting_items`; 0 `clearance`, `track_dangling`, `via_dangling` violations; 0 unconnected items except GND (GND pours fill in KiCad); every NEW net (CAN_TX, CAN_RX, CANH, CANL) fully routed; all `+12V*` tracks ≥ 2.5 mm (check with a small script over the .kicad_pcb text). Courtyard/silkscreen warnings are allowed. Run up to 3 fix rounds (move one part or one connector per round); if still failing after 3, STOP and write exactly which nets/pads fail.
6. Run `fab.sh` equivalent for the copy to write gerbers to `pcb12/gerbers/` and a `pcb12/bom.csv` with columns ref, value, footprint, qty for ONLY the changed/new parts plus a line "all other parts unchanged from v1.1". Do not invent prices.
