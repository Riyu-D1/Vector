   - `R11` 1 kΩ (same footprint as R5) from `MOTOR_EN` to `Q3_B`. `R12` 10 kΩ from `Q3_B` to GND (default OFF at boot).
   - Route `MOTOR_EN` U1A.15 → R11.
   - Change `J18` pin 1 from `+12V` to `+12V_MOT`. ADD `J20`, same Phoenix 2-pin as J18, pin 1 = `+12V_MOT`, pin 2 = GND (second motor output). K1.5 → J18.1 and J20.1 at 2.5 mm; K1.3 and K1.1 from the +12V rail at 2.5 mm (coil tap K1.1 may be 0.6 mm).
