3. **ESC joint driver** (`firmware/joint-driver`, SimpleFOC on B-G431B-ESC1): parameters for the Tarot 4008 330KV: pole pairs 7 [UNVERIFIED, count magnets], Kt = 60/(2π·330) ≈ 0.0289 N·m/A is for phase current; keep it as a param and mark [U]; phase current cap default 5 A [U, conservative until the bench test]; gear ratio 40 and efficiency 0.7 [U] as params so output torque = motor torque × 40 × eff.
