## Context
Read `order/STRESS_SANITY.md`, `cad3/STATUS-cad3.md`, `cad3/fea/`. Known weaknesses: whole-arm FEA used rigid-spider glue (stresses 2–4× optimistic); cycloidal teeth/pins only hand-checked (single-lobe bending SF<2 worst case); bolt/dowel load sharing assumed. The user wants the arm and actuators proven before ordering and is happy with heavy simulations and generative design. Material: PETG-CF (E=4000 MPa, 25 MPa cross-layer / 50 MPa along-layer, per repo convention) unless `cad3/PRINT_LIST.md` says otherwise. Rated torques: J2 3.5, J3 2.25 N·m; test at 2× (dynamic factor) and the shock case below.

## Tasks
1. **Cycloidal stage contact FEA (J2 and J3 actuator, 40:1).** Model the real cycloidal disc(s), ring pins, output pins/rollers and eccentric from `cad/act2/` (STEP in `act2/out/` or built from the params). C3D10 mesh, frictional or hard-contact (ccx `*CONTACT PAIR`, `*SURFACE INTERACTION` with friction μ=0.2 [U]) between disc lobes and ring pins at the worst phase angle(s) (at least 3 rotation positions), motor-side torque reflected through 40:1 to output torque of 3.5 N·m ×2. Report: peak contact pressure and von Mises in disc lobes, pin bending, SF vs printed limits (use the 25 MPa cross-layer limit for lobe bending unless the disc is printed flat so layers lie in the disc plane; state the print orientation assumption from `cad3/PRINT_LIST.md`), and also the deflection (backlash contribution in output degrees). If hard contact fails to converge, fall back to a tied-surface plus Hertz hand cross-check and SAY SO.
2. **Bolted whole-joint models.** Replace rigid spiders with real contact: J2 joint assembly (housing, flange, back spacer, link1 end, dowels, M3 screws as beam elements with preload approximated by `*BOUNDARY`-free tie plus `*CLOAD` on bolt shank nodes, or ccx preload), and the same for J3 and the J4 cradle with STS3250. Use `*CONTACT PAIR` (frictional) between mating faces. Apply full-arm reactions at 0.5 kg full reach ×2. Report the stress at every bolt hole and dowel hole, load share per bolt, SF.
3. **Shock and fatigue.** (a) Shock: arm released from horizontal with 0.5 kg through the J2 range, E-stop slam: estimate peak joint torque via a simple dynamic simulation (pure python, using `gravity_up1.py` masses/inertia and a 0.1 s stop), apply it as a quasi-static load and re-check SF. (b) Fatigue: estimate for printed PETG-CF the endurance limit (cite a real source; flag [U]), and a conservative cycle-count/lifetime for the bolt-hole and tooth stresses at 100% and 50% duty. 
4. **Whole-arm sanity run with credible glue**: only if time allows; otherwise state that you didn't. Do NOT claim whole-arm stress colours are realistic if rigid glue is used.
5. **Generative design (optional, only after 1–3 pass).** If a part is both over-built (SF > 6) and heavy, run a free topology optimisation (CalculiX with a simple SIMP script, or FreeCAD-compatible tool; if there's no working free tool, say so) on at most one link, keep every load path, and report mass saved. Do not apply it unless SF stays ≥ 3.
6. **Fix loop**: for every item with SF < 2, propose and apply a fix on the copy (wall/boss/fillet/insert/pin size/orientation/material), re-export STEP/STL into `verify/out/`, re-run that case once, and report before/after.
7. **Interactive 3D** with Fusion-style colour gradients (blue→red) as HTML (`pyvista` export_html or trame) plus PNGs into `verify/fea/` for: cycloidal disc/pins, J2 joint, J3 joint, J4 cradle.
8. `verify/STATUS-ver.md`: table of every case (mesh size, solver result OK/failed, max stress, location, SF), what was NOT possible, final verdict per part (PASS/MARGINAL/FAIL), [U] table. Commit, tag `ver-1-done`, push `arm-ver` with tags. STOP.
