# Stage D1 — Strengthen J2 and J4 so the arm carries a useful payload (design study on a COPY)

Real numbers only; flag anything unverified [U]; no invented prices or specs. Do not buy anything.

## Context
Current torque model (`software/robot-os/.../gravity.py`, rated J2 3.5, J3 2.25, J4/J5/J6 0.98 N·m; 80% rule) gives at the worst horizontal pose (q=[0,+70°,-70°,0,0,0]) a total payload of only 0.391 kg (J2-limited) and J4 allows 0.487 kg. Target: at least 0.5 kg at full reach with every joint at or below 80% of rated torque, without making the arm chunkier (user wants slim; if a joint is too weak, switch to a stronger servo/motor, never bulkier). Hardware is fixed otherwise: J2/J3 are 40:1 printed cycloidal with Tarot 4008 330KV + B-G431B-ESC1 over CAN; J4/J5/J6/gripper are STS3215; J1 STS3250.

## Tasks
1. Reproduce the baseline numbers by running the existing gravity model (quote them).
2. J4: evaluate options and rank them with the torque needed at 0.5 kg full reach: (a) a stronger Feetech servo that fits the J4 bracket envelope (check STS3250 and any other Feetech or comparable 12 V serial bus servo that is verifiable from a real datasheet or product page; give real stall torque, voltage, dimensions, mass, and a UK price with source URL and date checked, or mark [U]); (b) a small reduction stage or counterweight/spring gravity compensation on J4 using printed parts; (c) reducing the mass downstream of J4 (wrist and gripper masses from the BOM). Pick ONE recommendation with reasons.
3. J2: evaluate (a) a gas-spring or printed torsion-spring gravity-balance mechanism at the shoulder, with the spring rate and torque curve computed over the J2 range; (b) a lower motor current-limit assumption vs the real Tarot 4008 data (do not assume, flag [U]); (c) shaving mass from the upper-arm and elbow actuator. Pick ONE recommendation. Note that a spring should be a part available in the UK with a link and price, or mark [U].
4. Update the model on the COPY (`upgrade1/gravity_up1.py`) with the chosen changes and rerun: table of per-joint torque / rated / %utilisation at full reach for payloads 0, 0.25, 0.5 kg, plus `max_payload` at the worst pose. Add pytest tests for the new numbers and run them; paste the real pass count.
